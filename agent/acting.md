## Two-person interactions

In a screenplay: `[INTERACT: A <type> with=B]`; over MCP, the beat `{"kind":"interact","who":A,"other":B,"type":…}`.

- `handshake`: they shake hands
- `hug`: a warm hug
- `high-five`: a high five
- `slap`: the first slaps the other, who reels
- `punch`: a cartoon punch; the other is knocked back
- `push`: a shove; the other stumbles back
- `whisper`: the first whispers in the other's ear
- `kiss`: a kiss on the cheek (hearts)
- `comfort`: a comforting hand on the shoulder
- `give`: the first hands something over
- `bow`: they bow to each other
- `toast`: they raise their glasses (clink)
- `dance`: they dance together
- `argue`: a heated argument: finger-wagging and fist-shaking
- `scare`: the first jumps out — boo! — the other leaps in fright
- `tickle`: the first tickles the other, who laughs helplessly
- `point-at`: the first points at the other
- `wave-goodbye`: they wave goodbye
- `chase`: the second runs away, the first runs after
- `lead`: they walk off together, side by side
- `collar-grab`: the first grabs the other by the collar and yells in his face
- `arm-around`: an arm around the other's shoulders, matey
- `nudge`: an elbow in the ribs: "eh? eh?"
- `deadpan-look`: they turn and stare at each other, deadpan (the joke lands)
